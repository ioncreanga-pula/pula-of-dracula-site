Cookies

This site does not use cookies. That is why you see no consent banner.

What the site does not have

  • cookies of any kind;
  • traffic analytics;
  • ads, tracking pixels or social media buttons;
  • fonts or scripts loaded from other servers: everything comes from fagus.ro.

What is stored in your browser

One thing only: the theme you choose. When you press the theme button in the header, the browser remembers your choice in its local storage (localStorage), under the name fagus-theme, with the value light or dark.

  • It is not a cookie and it is not sent anywhere: it stays on your device.
  • It contains nothing that identifies you.
  • If you never press the button, nothing is stored and the site follows your system theme.

How to remove it

In your browser settings, clear the data stored for fagus.ro. The site will go back to your system theme.

For the data sent through the contact form, see the Privacy policy.
