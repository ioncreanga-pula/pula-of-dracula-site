Privacy policy

In short: I only collect what you write in the contact form, and I only use it to reply to you.

Who the controller is

What I collect and why

The contact form sends me what you fill in: your name, your email address or phone number, what you are interested in and your message. I use it only to reply to you and, if you ask, to prepare a quote.

Along with the message, the hosting provider records technical data such as your IP address and browser type, in order to deliver the site and to protect against spam.

Legal basis

Your consent, given by ticking the box in the form (GDPR art. 6(1)(a)). You can withdraw it at any time by writing to stefan_fagu@yahoo.com; withdrawing does not affect what was done before.

How long I keep the data

[TERMEN PĂSTRARE] from our last exchange of messages, after which I delete it. If we end up working together, accounting documents are kept for as long as the law requires.

Who else has access

The site is hosted, and the form is processed, by Netlify, Inc., a provider based in the United States, acting as a processor. This means the form data reaches servers outside the European Union.

The safeguards for this transfer: Netlify is certified under the EU–U.S. Data Privacy Framework, and its data processing agreement includes the European Commission's standard contractual clauses. See Netlify's privacy policy for details.

I do not sell your data or pass it on to anyone else.

Your rights

At any time you can ask me to tell you what data I hold about you and give you a copy, to correct it, to delete it, to restrict its use, or to hand it over in a format you can take elsewhere. You can object to the processing and withdraw your consent. Write to stefan_fagu@yahoo.com; I reply within one month at most.

If you believe your data is not being handled properly, you can lodge a complaint with ANSPDCP, the Romanian data protection authority, or with the authority in your own EU country.

Cookies and data stored in your browser

The site uses no cookies and no traffic analytics. If you choose the light or dark theme, that preference is saved locally in your browser and never reaches me. See the Cookies page for details.
