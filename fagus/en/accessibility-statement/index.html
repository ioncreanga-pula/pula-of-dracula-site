Accessibility statement

I want fagus.ro to work for everyone, whatever their eyesight, the way they navigate or the device they use.

The standard aimed for

The site is built to meet WCAG 2.2, level AA (the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines).

Conformance status

The checks carried out so far have not turned up any failure of WCAG 2.2 AA. The evaluation is not complete, though (see the limitations below), so I do not claim full conformance.

What I paid particular attention to:

  • two themes, light and dark, both with a contrast of at least 4.5:1 for text;
  • the whole site can be used from the keyboard alone, with visible focus and a “Skip to content” link;
  • every form field has a label;
  • pages adapt to 200% zoom and to narrow screens;
  • animations are switched off if you have asked for that in your device settings.

Known limitations

  • The site has not yet been tested with screen readers, or by people with disabilities.
  • In the case study, the results table scrolls sideways on narrow screens. It can be scrolled from the keyboard as well.
  • Form error messages are the ones shown by the browser and may differ from one browser to another.
  • The case study links to external pages (anaf.ro) whose accessibility is outside my control.

How it was evaluated

Self-assessment carried out on 1 October 2026: automated testing with axe-core 4.13.0 in both themes, on desktop and phone, plus checks of keyboard navigation and of the layout when zoomed.
